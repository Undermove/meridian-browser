How to export your Arc Easels before December 22

Easels live in your Arc account, so after December 22 you can't open them, and no other browser imports them. Exporting takes a minute.

Based on Arc's help center as checked on November 24, 2026.

Export all Easels at once

  1. In Arc, open your Library, then select Easels.
  2. Select Export All Easels.
  3. Choose where to save the file, then select Export.

Arc saves a ZIP file named Arc Easels with the date and time of the export. Inside, every Easel you made is a web page, plus a page to browse them all in any browser. Keep the ZIP somewhere outside Arc, such as a cloud drive.

What the export leaves out

  • Easels other people shared with you. Only Easels you created are in the ZIP. If someone shared one you care about, ask them to export it, or save it as an image (below).
  • Who you shared your Easels with.

Save one Easel as an image

Open the Easel, select the Share icon in the bottom-right corner, then Share Via… to save it as a PNG.

Why now

Arc's help center says Easels are stored with your Arc account, so you won't be able to get to them after the sunset, and they don't come over to Dia either. Arc accounts are deactivated on December 22, 2026.

The rest of what to save is in the full Arc backup checklist, including your Boosts, which Arc can't export at all.

Sources

  • Arc Help Center: How Do I Export My Data from Arc? ("How do I save my Easels?")
