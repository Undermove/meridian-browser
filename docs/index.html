Questions

Still stuck? Ask on Discord or open an issue on GitHub.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. Meridian is free and open source under the MIT license. No subscription, no account, no ads.

What comes over from Arc?

Everything that matters: spaces, pinned tabs, folders, favorites, history, boosts, saved passwords and cards. You stay signed in too, because Meridian copies Arc's cookies into the matching profiles (on a Mac, after you allow the keychain prompt). Nothing leaves your computer and Arc isn't changed. On Windows, quit Arc first. Coming from Chrome? File › Import from Chrome does the same for your profiles.

Which computers does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and newer) and Windows 10 and 11. Intel Macs and Linux aren't supported yet.

What does Meridian know about me?

Nothing. There is no account, no server and no telemetry. AI features talk only to the AI you connect.

Why does Windows warn me about it?

Windows shows that warning for every app without a Microsoft certificate, and we don't have ours yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Macs show no warning: Meridian is signed and checked by Apple.

What's missing?

No sync and no password manager of its own yet, and the Windows build has had less testing than the Mac one. We'd rather tell you now.
