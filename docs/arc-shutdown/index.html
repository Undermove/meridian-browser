Arc shuts down on December 22: what happens and how to keep your data

The Browser Company is ending Arc. Here are the dates, what stops working, and how to take your spaces, tabs and logins with you.

Facts checked on November 20, 2026, against arc.net, Arc's help center and 9to5Mac. Sources are at the end.

The timeline

  • October 22, 2026The Browser Company announces that Arc will be sunset on December 22, 2026. New Arc accounts can no longer be created.
  • November 23, 2026Arc Search on iPhone and Android loses its AI features (such as Browse for Me), and you can no longer sign in or sync tabs there.
  • December 15, 2026The last day to ask The Browser Company for a copy of the personal data tied to your Arc account, or to ask them to delete it. This is about your account data, not the export from the app.
  • December 22, 2026Arc stops working on macOS and Windows, and accounts are deactivated. On Android, Arc Search is removed from Google Play and from devices.
  • Within 30 days after deactivationPersonal data related to the account is deleted. From December 22, The Browser Company says it can no longer find or retrieve Arc user data for requests.

What you lose

  • Arc itself on Mac and Windows: the sidebar, spaces, pinned tabs and everything synced through your Arc account.
  • Easels are tied to your Arc account and can't be opened after the sunset. Arc offers a ZIP export of all of them, so save it before December 22.
  • Boosts can't be exported from Arc.
  • Dia, The Browser Company's other browser, doesn't have Air Traffic Control, Boosts, Easels, Little Arc, Peek, TypeTravel or Instant Links, according to Arc's help center.

How to export from Arc (before December 22)

  • macOS: Arc › Settings… › Advanced › Export Data.
  • Windows: in Settings, under Advanced, find Export Spaces and tabs and choose Export Data.
  • Export your Easels too, from Arc's Easel export, as a ZIP.

Arc's export gives you a file. Another browser can also read Arc's data straight from your computer while Arc is still installed, and that includes things the export file doesn't, such as logins.

Moving your sidebar to Meridian

Meridian is a free, open-source browser built for people who loved Arc. It runs on macOS and Windows. Its import reads Arc's data on your computer and brings over your spaces with their colors, pinned tabs, folders, favorites, open tabs, logins, saved passwords, history and Boosts, in one click. Nothing leaves your computer, and Arc isn't changed. Do it before December 22, while Arc is still installed. What exactly comes over.
