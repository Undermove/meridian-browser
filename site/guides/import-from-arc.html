How to import

  1. Install Meridian (macOS or Windows).
  2. The welcome tour offers Import from Arc the first time Meridian starts. Later, it's in Settings.
  3. On a Mac, allow the keychain prompt. It lets Meridian read the key Arc encrypts its logins with. On Windows, quit Arc first: while it's open, Arc locks the files that hold your logins.

Nothing leaves your computer, and Arc isn't changed. You can keep using it until it stops working on December 22.

What comes over

  • Spaces, with their colors, icons and names. Each Arc profile gets a Meridian profile, so a work space keeps its own cookies and logins.
  • Pinned tabs and folders, in their order. Arc's split views and Easels come over as folders of their tabs.
  • Favorites and each space's open Today tabs.
  • Logins: Arc's cookies are copied into the matching profiles, so sites you were signed in to stay signed in.
  • Saved passwords and cards, form entries and addresses.
  • History, Arc's archive and site permissions.
  • Boosts and Air Traffic Control rules.
  • Extensions from the Chrome Web Store, installed again in the right profile (on macOS).

What doesn't come over

  • On Windows, Arc's extensions don't come over yet. Install them again from the Chrome Web Store.
  • The contents of an Easel (drawings, text, captures) stay in Arc. Only its links come over, as a folder.
  • Arc Sync and your Arc account: Meridian has no account or sync of its own yet. Import on each computer.
Coming from Google Chrome instead? File › Import from Chrome… brings each Chrome profile over as a space: bookmarks as pinned tabs and folders, open tabs, sign-ins, passwords, history and extensions. On Windows, Chrome locks its sign-ins to itself, so you sign in to your sites once more there. Passwords still come over.
