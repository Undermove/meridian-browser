What Arc says

Arc's own help page answers "Can I save my Boosts?" with: "No. Boosts can't be exported from Arc", and adds that you won't be able to get to them after Arc is sunset on December 22, 2026. So the only way to keep a Boost is to copy it out yourself while Arc still works.

1. Find all your Boosts

In Arc, press ⌘T and type View Boosts. That lists the Boosts on this computer. Go through the list and decide which ones you'd miss. Boosts you only turned on for colors or fonts are quick to remake; the ones with your own CSS or JavaScript are the ones to save.

2. Copy the code of each Boost you wrote

  1. Open the site the Boost is for, then open the Boost in Arc's Boosts editor.
  2. Copy what's in the CSS and JavaScript parts into a plain text file. Name it after the site, for example github.com.css and github.com.js.
  3. Keep the files outside Arc: a cloud drive, a notes app or a backup disk.

For Boosts you made with Zap, write down what you removed (or take a screenshot of the site with and without the Boost), so you can zap the same things again.

3. Don't rely on share links

Arc can share a Boost as a link (⌘T, then Share Boost). Those links are served by Arc, so treat them as a way to send a Boost to a friend today, not as a backup for next year. Keep the text files from step 2.

4. Use them in your next browser

  • Any Chromium browser: put the CSS into a style extension such as Stylus, and the JavaScript into a userscript manager such as Violentmonkey.
  • Meridian has Boosts too, and its import reads your Arc Boosts from Arc's files on your computer while Arc is still installed. Even so, keep your own copy from step 2: whatever browser you pick, don't bet your favorite Boost on anyone's import.
