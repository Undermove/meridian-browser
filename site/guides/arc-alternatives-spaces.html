Where another browser is the better pick

  • You want Firefox's engine: Zen is the one here built on Firefox.
  • You use Linux: Zen, or Ardia's nightly builds. Meridian has no Linux build.
  • You want tabs synced between devices: Dia offers end-to-end encrypted sync, Crest has optional iCloud sync, and Yab says its sync is sealed on your Mac before it leaves. Meridian has no sync yet.
  • You want it on your iPhone too: Crest and Yab have iPhone betas, and Ardia has a TestFlight build. Meridian is desktop only.
  • You want the official route from The Browser Company: that's Dia, with built-in AI on its paid plans.
  • You want no AI at all: Ardia says it has none, on purpose.
  • You want a very small app: Yab runs on the WebKit already inside your Mac and is a 40 MB download.

Where Meridian fits

  • You want your Boosts and Air Traffic Control rules to come across too. The import covers spaces with colors, pinned tabs, folders, favorites, open tabs, logins, passwords and cards, history, Boosts and Air Traffic Control rules. See exactly what comes over.
  • You miss Arc's smaller features: Peek, Boosts, the Little window and Air Traffic Control are all there.
  • You're on Windows and want Arc import. Our Windows version has had less testing than the Mac one, so expect some rough edges.
  • You work with AI coding agents and want them in your browser. It's optional, and off by default.
