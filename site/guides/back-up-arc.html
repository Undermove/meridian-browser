The short checklist

  1. Export your spaces and tabs from Arc's settings.
  2. Export your Easels as a ZIP.
  3. Save your Boosts by hand, because Arc can't export them.
  4. Move your logins and passwords into your next browser while Arc is still installed.
  5. By December 15, ask The Browser Company for your account data if you want a copy.
  6. Keep Arc installed until you've checked that everything arrived.

1. Export spaces and tabs

  • macOS: Arc › Settings… › Advanced › Export Data.
  • Windows: in Settings, under Advanced, find Export Spaces and tabs and choose Export Data.

Keep the file somewhere outside Arc, such as a cloud drive or a backup disk. It doesn't include your logins.

2. Export Easels

Easels are tied to your Arc account and can't be opened after the sunset. Use Arc's Easel export to save all of them as a ZIP before December 22: how to export your Easels.

3. Save your Boosts

Arc has no export for Boosts. If you wrote your own, open each one and copy its code into a file. Some browsers can read them from Arc on your computer. Meridian's import does, so check what your next browser says about Boosts. Step by step: how to save your Arc Boosts.

4. Move logins and passwords

The export file doesn't carry your logins, so they need a browser that can read them from Arc on your computer while Arc is still there. Check what your next browser imports before you rely on it. Our comparison of Arc alternatives lists what each one says it brings over.

In Meridian, the import copies logins, saved passwords and cards, history, Boosts and your sidebar. Nothing leaves your computer, and Arc isn't changed.

5. Ask for your account data by December 15

December 15, 2026 is the last day to ask The Browser Company for a copy of the personal data tied to your Arc account, or to ask them to delete it. This is separate from the export in the app. From December 22, they say they can no longer find or retrieve Arc user data for requests, and personal data related to the account is deleted within 30 days after deactivation.

6. Check before you let go

Open your new browser next to Arc and go through your spaces, your most-used logins and any folder you'd hate to lose. Do this well before December 22, so there's still time to fix a gap. For the full timeline, see what happens on December 22.
